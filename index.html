I'm Ajit Bhagwan Bedwal — a Computer Engineering student at ISBM College of Engineering, Pune. My path ran from Saint Monica School in Vaijapur, through a Diploma in Computer Engineering at MIT Polytechnic, Rotegaon (84.74%), into my B.Tech (Sep 2024 – present). At every step, one thing stayed constant: I learn technology best by building things with it.

What genuinely excites me is the moment an idea starts working — a desktop assistant that answers spoken commands, sensors wired into an ESP32 that behave exactly as the Python code expects, a farming platform that puts useful knowledge in a farmer's hands. I'm passionate about Python and software development, and I gravitate towards work where software meets hardware.

I care about building things that are private, reliable and genuinely useful: Jarvis AI processes tasks locally by design, and Pure Planting was built to make organic-farming knowledge easy to reach. Hackathons, poster presentations and constant exploration of new tools keep me growing.

Privacy-first engineering Hands-on learning Real-world impact Consistent growth

What makes me different: I'm equally at home writing Python for a Gemini-powered assistant as I am wiring sensors into an ESP32 and debugging the link between them. Software and hardware — end to end.
